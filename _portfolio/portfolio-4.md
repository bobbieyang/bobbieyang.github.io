excerpt: "AI is reshaping conventional chip design ecosystem (e.g. EDA tools and IPs) and creating new opportunities for industry–academic collaboration. This research investigates AI-assisted chip physical design frameworks to reduce placement-and-routing (P&R) iteration cycles, mitigate routing congestion, and co-optimize PPA. It focuses on post-routing timing prediction, routing congestion analysis, and static timing analysis (STA)-guided optimization to resolve setup and hold violations, improve worst negative slack (WNS), recommend targeted engineering change orders (ECOs), and guide floorplan refinement. All AI-driven optimization recommendations are validated using industry-standard signoff toolchains. In the frontend, the research evaluates LLM-generated RTL designs for functional correctness and implementation viability. It aims to investigate a hierarchical approach to LLM-assisted hardware generation that uses pre-verified IP cores and building blocks to reduce functional errors and timing violations while maintaining PPA. <br/><img src='/files/projects/chip design flow.png' width='600' style='height:auto;'>"

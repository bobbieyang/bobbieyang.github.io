title: "Trustworthy Quantum Verification Framework for Bimolecular Quantum Dynamics"
excerpt: "Accurate simulation of nonequilibrium quantum dynamics is essential for studying biomedical processes, such as electronic excitation in photodynamic cancer therapy (PDT) and spin dynamics in enzyme-catalyzed reactions. Existing simulation methods, including tensor networks and Pauli propagation, can face significant approximation errors as quantum entanglement or operator complexity increases. My research aims to develop a trustworthy, hardware-accelerated quantum verification framework, enabling reliable studies of complex biomolecular quantum dynamics. Targeting ASIC operation at 1 GHz, another research direction will develop quantum error correction (QEC) decoders and electronic control interfaces with precise cycle-level timing.
